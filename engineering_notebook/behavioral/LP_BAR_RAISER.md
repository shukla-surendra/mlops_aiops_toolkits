# The LP / Bar-Raiser Company — Behavioral Interview Prep

Read `FRAMEWORK.md` first for the STAR(L) structure and how to build your story bank —
this doc is just the mapping and mechanics for a company that runs on a large set of
published Leadership Principles graded by a dedicated bar raiser.

## How the round is actually run

A dedicated **bar raiser** — not a member of your would-be team — sits in on the loop
specifically to hold the Leadership Principles (LP) bar independent of whether the hiring
team likes you, and can veto an otherwise-positive loop. Individual interviewers are often
assigned specific LPs to probe and will drill follow-ups ("what exactly did *you* do,
step by step") until they isolate your individual contribution from the team's. Nearly
every question, technical or behavioral, can come with an LP-flavored follow-up.

## The published Leadership Principles (condensed)

Use this as prompts to sort your brain-dumped stories against, not as a checklist to
write new fake ones to fill gaps.

| LP | What it's really asking |
|---|---|
| Customer Obsession | Did you make a call that cost you something short-term to protect the customer? |
| Ownership | Did you fix something that "wasn't your job," or think beyond your immediate task? |
| Invent and Simplify | Did you remove complexity/steps rather than add a clever solution? |
| Are Right, A Lot | A time your judgment call under uncertainty turned out correct — and how you knew. |
| Learn and Be Curious | A time you deliberately went outside your existing skill set. |
| Hire and Develop the Best | Mentoring, raising the bar on a team, giving hard feedback well. |
| Insist on the Highest Standards | A time you rejected "good enough" and it was the right call. |
| Think Big | A time you proposed something bigger in scope than what was asked. |
| Bias for Action | A time you made a reversible decision fast instead of over-analyzing. |
| Frugality | Doing more with less — not just cost, also time/people/infra. |
| Earn Trust | Delivering bad news, admitting a mistake, disagreeing respectfully. |
| Dive Deep | A time surface-level data was misleading and you dug in to find the real cause. |
| Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit | Pushed back on a decision, lost the argument, committed fully anyway. |
| Deliver Results | A time you hit a hard deadline despite obstacles — with numbers. |
| Strive to be Earth's Best Employer | Improving team culture/process for others, not just yourself. |
| Success and Scale Bring Broad Responsibility | Considered second-order/external impact of a decision. |

## Company-specific failure mode

An answer that doesn't obviously map to a single LP, or that vaguely maps to five LPs at
once without clearly demonstrating any of them, reads worse here than a narrower but
sharper answer — the bar raiser is listening for a specific, nameable principle in your
own retelling, not a generically positive story. When you rehearse, practice naming out
loud *which* LP a story targets before you tell it — if you can't name one, it's not
ready.

## Prep checklist specific to this loop

- For each of your ~10 story-bank entries, tag the 2-3 LPs it can honestly answer (see
  `STORY_BANK_TEMPLATE.md`).
- Make sure "Earn Trust" and "Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit" are each covered by a
  *different* story if possible — these two get asked disproportionately often and
  reusing one story for both is a common and noticeable pattern.
- Practice the "what exactly did *you* do" follow-up specifically — have the next level
  of detail ready for every story, not just the summary version.
